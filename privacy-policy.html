If you email us, we process the information you provide, such as your email address, name and message, to answer your request. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual steps, otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. 6. Our games

Our games do not require you to create an account with SCIZARD. Unless stated otherwise in a game, game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Depending on the game, the following services may be used. Each store listing states which data a game collects.

Advertising: Google AdMob

Our mobile games may show ads via Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited. AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to deliver, measure and, with your consent, personalise ads.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google’s consent message when the game starts. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG); otherwise non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice in the game’s privacy settings or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

Our mobile games may use Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand how players use the game, for example which levels are played and how long sessions last. This involves an app instance ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and in-game events. Analytics is only used with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG), which you can withdraw at any time in the game’s privacy settings.

Crash reporting: Firebase Crashlytics

To find and fix errors, our mobile games may use Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited. When the game crashes, a report containing device information, operating system version, app state and a Crashlytics installation identifier is sent to Google. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in stable, error-free games). Crash reports are deleted after 90 days.

In-app purchases

Some games may offer in-app purchases. Payments are processed entirely by the respective app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details; we only receive the confirmation needed to deliver your purchase (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Games on third-party platforms

Some of our games are published on platforms such as Roblox. For those games, the platform operator is responsible for account and gameplay data under its own privacy policy.

7. Recipients and data transfers

The service providers named above process data on our behalf or as independent controllers. Google and Netlify may process data in the USA. Such transfers are based on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Article 45 GDPR) or Standard Contractual Clauses (Article 46 GDPR).

8. Children

Our games are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by statutory retention obligations. Data processed on the basis of consent is deleted once you withdraw your consent, unless another legal basis applies.

To exercise your rights, email info@scizard.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority responsible for us: Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen, Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany.
